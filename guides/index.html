User guides

For SiteProof 0.9.0 (build 10) · November 2026

සිංහලෙන් කියවන්න (Read in Sinhala)

SiteProof helps a family follow the building of their house in Sri Lanka: photos verified on site, stages, bills, payment requests and the budget. Everyone in a project has a role. Your role decides what you see and what you can do, so there is one guide for each.

Choose your role

Owner

You are building the house. You see everything, decide on every payment and invite the team.

Co-owner

You help the owner run the project: you see everything, approve payments and invite people.

Family member on site

You live near the site. You post photos and bills, set the site location and ask for payments.

Contractor

You build the house. You post photos, add your bills and ask for payments. You don't see the owner's budget.

Designer

Architect, draughtsman or engineer. You share drawings, check stages and answer questions.

Viewer

You follow the progress, for example a parent. You see the photos and stages, never money.

Not sure which role you have? On the home screen, each project's card shows your role (your own house shows none). A project that opens on the Overview also shows Your role there; the family member on site and the contractor open on Today instead.

Before you start

  • The app: SiteProof is an Android app. While it is being tested, only invited testers can install it from Google Play. An iPhone app is planned.
  • Signing in: with your email address (SiteProof emails you a 6-digit code) or with Continue with Google. There is no password.
  • Joining a project: the owner sends you a 6-character code, usually on WhatsApp. In the app, tap ⋮ on the home screen, then Join with a code.
  • Language: English or Sinhala. You choose on the first screen and can change it any time in Settings.
  • Light or dark: Settings → Appearance: As my phone (the usual), Light or Dark. Light is easiest to read outdoors in bright sun.
  • Cost: only the owner pays. Everyone else uses SiteProof free. While SiteProof is being tested, every project has Plus free.
  • Help in the app: Settings → Help: questions and answers, chat with us on WhatsApp, report a problem, or rate SiteProof on Google Play.
