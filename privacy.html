Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

SiteProof, a business being registered in Sri Lanka, runs the SiteProof app and is responsible for the personal data described here ("we", "us"). SiteProof helps families who are building a house in Sri Lanka keep a verified record of the work: site photos, bills, payment approvals and live checks. Often the owner lives abroad and a family member or contractor is on site.

This policy covers the SiteProof apps for Android and iPhone, and this website. It applies to everyone who uses them: owners and co-owners, the family member on site, contractors, designers and viewers.

A SiteProof project is shared by the people the owner invites. What you add to a project (a photo, a bill, a comment) is seen by its other members, as described in "Who can see it".

How we use it

We use your data only to run SiteProof for you and your project team:

  • Keep the project's record: show photos, bills, payments, comments and documents to the project's members.
  • Check site photos: compare the photo's GPS position with the site pin, use our server's time rather than the phone's, and spot repeated photos and simulated locations. The result is a "Verified on site" badge or a warning.
  • Send notifications: new photos, bills, payment requests, live checks and changes, instantly or in your daily summary.
  • Run live checks: ask the person on site for a photo now, and tell the owner if it is missed.
  • Read a bill, when you ask: if you tap "Fill in from the photo", the bill photo is sent to an AI service that reads the shop, date, items and total, and fills them in for you to check. Nothing is saved until you save the bill.
  • Write the weekly summary: for the owners and co-owners of a project on Plus, every Monday, an AI service writes a few lines about the week from the project's records: its name, its stages, how many photos and bills there were and the amounts. It gets no photos and no one's name or phone number, and every number it writes is checked against the records.
  • Check a payment request: when someone on site asks for a payment on a project on Plus, an AI service writes a few lines about it for the owners and co-owners from the project's records: the amount, the stage, its budget and payments, the agreed payment it is for in the contract's payment schedule, and how many photos there were and their checks. It gets no photos, no note and no one's name or phone number, every number it writes is checked against the records, and it never decides for you.
  • Read a contract, when an owner asks: if an owner or co-owner taps "Read it from the contract", that contract (a PDF or photo from the project's documents) is sent to an AI service with the names of the project's stages. It reads the contractor's name, the total and the agreed payments, and fills in the payment schedule for them to check. A contract may also show the names, addresses or other details of the people who signed it; the AI is asked only for the payments. Nothing is saved until they save the schedule.
  • Fix problems: crash reports show us what broke, on which kind of phone.
  • Answer you: when you contact us for help or a deletion request.

We never sell your data, use it for advertising, show a site location on any public page, or let other projects see your project. AI is used in four places only, through a provider that does not train its models on your data: reading a bill when you ask, reading a contract when an owner asks, and, for owners, the weekly summary and the check of a payment request. Before we add any other AI feature, we will update this policy.

Who can see it

Only the members of a project see what is in it. Within a project:

  • Everyone in the team sees the photos, the site location, stages, comments and documents.
  • Everyone in the team sees each other's name and role. If you signed in with your phone number, the people in your projects see that number too, so they can call you or message you on WhatsApp from the app.
  • Budgets are hidden from contractors and viewers; designers see them only if the owner allows it.
  • The payment schedule from the contract is seen by the people who see the budget, and by the contractor.
  • A person who asked for a payment can see its slip, even without seeing the budget.
  • Photos and files are kept in private storage and shown through links that stop working after 1 hour.

Your choices and rights

You decide what the app may use on your phone, and you can see, correct or delete your data:

  • Camera: needed to take site photos, bills and payment slips in the app.
  • Location: used only while you take a site photo or set the site pin. You can refuse it; your photos then show a "No location" warning instead of "Verified on site".
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings, or choose a daily summary in Settings → Notifications.
  • See and correct: change your name, language and notification settings in the app. To change your phone number or email, contact us.
  • Get a copy: Settings → Download my data gives you a file with your details and everything you added. Photo files stay in the app; the file lists when and where each was taken.
  • Leave a project: ask its owner to remove you, or delete your account.
  • Complain: contact us first; you can also complain to Sri Lanka's Data Protection Authority.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Delete my account. It happens straight away and cannot be undone. What is deleted and what stays, and how to ask without the app, is on Delete your SiteProof account.

Security

These are the homes of families whose breadwinner is often abroad, so we protect them carefully. Data travels encrypted between your phone and our servers. Database rules decide who sees each record, and automatic tests check those rules before every release. Photos and files sit in private storage, reached only through links that expire after 1 hour.

Children and photos of people

SiteProof is not meant for anyone under 18. Please don't photograph children or neighbours unless it is needed to show the work.
