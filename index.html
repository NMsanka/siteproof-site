Photos verified on site

See the site as it is, not as you’re told

Every photo is taken in the SiteProof app. Each one gets a badge you can trust: verified on site, with a warning, or flagged. Nobody can change it afterwards.

  • Taken at the site, at the time it says
  • Compare the same spot day by day, with the camera’s ghost to line it up
  • Ask for a live check: a new photo from the site, now

Budget and bills

Every rupee, accounted for

Bills go against each stage with a photo of the bill. Your budget is split the way building really works: the contract with your builder, and everything else you buy.

  • What’s spent and what’s left, stage by stage
  • Charts of spending over time and where the money goes
  • Snap a bill and it’s read for you Plus

Payment requests

See the facts before you pay

When the builder needs money, they ask in the app, with photos of the work. You see what it leaves of the stage’s budget and the agreed payment, and you decide.

  • A notification the moment a request comes in

User guides

A guide for everyone in the project

Step by step, with screenshots, in English and in සිංහල.

Owner

You’re building the house. You see everything, decide on every payment and invite the team.

Co-owner

You run the project with the owner: you see everything and approve payments too.

Family member on site

You live near the site. You post photos and bills, and set the site location.

Contractor

You build the house. Post the work, add your bills and ask for payments.

Designer

Architect, draughtsman or engineer. Share drawings, check stages and answer questions.

Viewer

You follow the progress, for example a parent: the photos and stages, never the money.
