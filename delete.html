Delete your SiteProof account

SiteProof, by SiteProof (Sri Lanka)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SiteProof and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

It happens straight away and cannot be undone.

If you own a project that other people are in, hand it over first: Team tab → tap a co-owner, the family member on site or a viewer → Make owner. Or remove the other members. The app tells you who is still there.

Without the app

Email nmsankapadma@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, or tell us the phone number you sign in with. We confirm it is you, then delete your account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Projects only you are in, with all their photos and files.
  • Your name, phone numbers, push notification ids and notifications.
  • Your phone number and email are removed from sign-in, and you are signed out on every phone.
  • You leave every other project.

What is kept, and for how long

  • What you added to other people's projects (photos, bills, payment approvals) stays there, shown as "Former member", because it is their record of their house. It is kept as long as their project exists.
  • Backups hold a copy for up to 7 days, then it is overwritten.
  • Crash reports, which carry only a random id, are deleted by Firebase after 90 days.
